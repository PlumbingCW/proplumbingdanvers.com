Tankless Water Heater Service in Danvers, MA

A tankless unit heats water as it flows, so there is no tank to run dry, but there is a narrow heat exchanger that scale loves to clog. This page covers tankless water heater service in Danvers, MA: flushing, repair and new installs.

Most service calls trace to three things: mineral scale, a gas or venting limit, or a sensor that has drifted. We find which before we replace anything.

The Flush Your Tankless Unit Is Asking For

A flush circulates a mild descaling solution, usually white vinegar or a manufacturer-approved product, through the heat exchanger and out of the unit using a small pump and hoses. It removes the mineral crust that builds on the inside of the narrow passages. The job is done through isolation valves on the water lines, which is why a good installation includes them.

Signs it is due include a fluctuating temperature, a slower flow at the tap, a unit that fires and shuts off, or an error code related to flow or overheating. Skipping flushes can shorten the life of the exchanger. If you only need a flush, you do not need a new heater, and we say so.

Water turns lukewarm

Scale on the exchanger blocks heat transfer, so the unit cannot hit its setpoint at normal flow.

Cold sandwich effect

Hot water surges, then goes cold, then returns. Scale or a dirty inlet filter can cause the swings.

Error code on the display

Many codes mean flow, ignition or exhaust trouble. Write down the code before you reset it.

Noise or kettling

A popping or rumbling sound suggests scale where water boils in a hot spot.

Why Testing Your Tap Matters Here

The Town of Danvers says white scaling or hard deposits on faucets and shower heads may come from calcium carbonate. That is the same mineral that coats a heat exchanger. The Town does not publish a hardness number, so you cannot assume a figure for your house.

The plumbing code table in 248 CMR 10.14 lists a residential hot water range of 120F minimum and 130F maximum, with an exception noted, which is the standard to keep in mind when setting output temperature.

Test your tap with a simple hardness kit, or ask the Water Division for its annual water quality report. If your water leaves scale on the showerhead, set a flush schedule sooner rather than later. If it does not, flushes can be spaced out, and you save the cost.

Tankless Troubleshooting: What We Check First

We start with the cheap and common items. The inlet water filter screen can clog with debris. The flow sensor needs minimum flow to start the burner, so a low-flow faucet may never trigger the unit. A shower mixing valve that allows cross flow between hot and cold can also confuse the sensor.

Then we look at gas pressure under load, the vent for blockage and the condensate drain on high-efficiency models. Condensing units produce acidic water, and a blocked drain trips them offline. If a unit leaks, we check the exchanger, the unions and the condensate line before blaming the whole heater. A tank-style failure is covered under water heater repair.

Gas Line And Venting Needs

A tankless burner uses far more gas for a short time than a tank burner does. The pipe that fed a 40-gallon tank may be too small, which causes ignition faults and low output. Sizing depends on the pipe length, the other appliances on the line and the unit rating.

Venting also differs. Many units use sealed plastic vent or stainless pipe through a sidewall, not a chimney, and the manufacturer sets length and clearance limits. For a conversion from a tank, see our guide on water heater installation, which covers permits and fuel questions in more detail.

When A Tankless Unit Is Worth Choosing

  • You want continuous hot water for a large family or a soaking tub
  • You are short on floor space in a basement or closet
  • You can supply the gas line and venting without major rework
  • You are willing to flush it on a schedule

If a tank has failed and you are comparing, our water heater replacement page explains tank sizing and code items. Tankless units are not always cheaper over time, and their value depends on the work around them.
