Replace Or Repair: The Quick Test

Ask two things. Is the water coming from the tank body, not a fitting? Is the heater near the end of its usual service life? If the answer to the first is yes, replacement is the only option, because a corroded steel shell cannot be sealed.

Under 248 CMR 10.14, the relief valve discharge must be full-size with no shut-off and end at a fixture or within 12 inches of the floor, a standard we install to.

If a single part has failed and the tank is sound, water heater repair usually costs far less than a new unit. We lay out both choices at your house and let you pick. Where the tank has a rusted base, a failed anode or repeat repairs behind it, putting more money into it rarely pays off.

Sizing The Tank To Your Household

A tank that is too small runs out during back-to-back showers, while one that is too large wastes energy keeping extra water hot. The number to look for is first-hour rating, which shows how much hot water the heater delivers in its first hour of heavy use. It matters more than the tank volume printed on the label.

Count the people, bathrooms and big users such as a soaking tub or a dishwasher that draws hot water. About 69.5 percent of Danvers homes predate 1980, so a house that has added a bath or two over the decades may need a bigger heater than the one it started with. Tell us how mornings go and we size from that.

Gas, Electric Or Something Else

Replacing like for like is the simplest path. A gas tank into a gas tank reuses the venting and gas line, while a switch to electric needs a dedicated circuit and a different layout. A move to a tankless water heater changes the gas line size and venting, which is why it is a larger job.

If you are changing fuel or moving the heater, our water heater installation page covers that work in more detail. Gas in town is supplied by National Grid, and electricity comes from Danvers Electric, so a fuel change may involve a call to the utility.

Permit Rules In Plain Terms

Massachusetts requires a permit before plumbing is installed, altered, removed, replaced or repaired. A water heater swap falls under the general replaced rule, and permits go only to licensed plumbers. Danvers Inspectional Services, at 1 Sylvan Street, handles local permits, but no Danvers page spells out when one is needed, so we confirm for your job.

Anyone can check a plumber's license through the state's online license lookup. Only a master or journeyman licensed by the Board of State Examiners of Plumbers and Gas Fitters may do the work. That protects you if something goes wrong later, since an unpermitted heater can cause trouble at a home sale or an insurance claim.

Relief Valve And Expansion Tank Rules

Under 248 CMR 10.14, a storage heater needs a properly sized temperature and pressure relief valve within 12 inches of the top of a vertical tank. The discharge pipe must be full size with no shut-off valve, ending at a fixture or within 12 inches of the floor. We do not reuse a tired valve on a new tank.

A thermal expansion tank is allowed under the code, and one is required near the meter when a check valve or backflow preventer makes your plumbing a closed system. Danvers water rules call for supply protection and backflow testing twice a year. No check valve may sit in the cold supply to the water heater itself. We check your setup so heating does not spike pressure.

How Replacement Day Goes

Shut down and drain

We close the supply, kill the fuel or power and drain the old tank to a floor drain or hose line.

Disconnect and remove

Gas, water, vent and electrical connections come apart and the old tank leaves the house.

Set and connect

The new tank goes on a level base or pan, with new flex lines, a relief valve, discharge pipe and any expansion tank.

Test and explain

We fill, purge air, check for leaks and combustion, set the temperature and show you the shutoffs.

Most swaps finish in a single visit when parts match. A change of fuel, venting or location can add time. For temperature, the code range for residential hot water is 120 F minimum to 130 F maximum, with a stated exception, so we set it there.
